## How to merge

Overlay the pack onto the repo at the same relative paths:

- `systems/...` files go to `res://systems/...` (same subfolders: `campaign/`, `cast/`, `difficulty/`, `overworld/`, `pokedex/`, `rules/`, `transportation/`, `vexa/`).
- `data/...` files go to `res://data/` (`competitive_sets.json`, `mdx_mapping.json`, `mdx_voices.json`).
- `tests/...` files go to `res://tests/`.
- `tools/...` are the offline builders that produced the data files; they are not loaded by the game.
- `world/overworld_loop.gd` and `core/save_manager.gd` (Phase 3 item E) go to `res://world/` and `res://core/` — they are updated copies of existing repo files (habitat-encounter wiring; the additive `habitat_rotation` save key), not new files.
- `CANON_DECISIONS.md` is documentation; keep it at the repo root or with the project docs.
- This file and `MANIFEST.json` are pack bookkeeping and do not need to be merged.

No existing repo file is replaced by this pack except where a path already held an earlier copy of the same integration file; all other content is additive.

## Region locks and gym scaling (Riley canon, 2026-11-22, Phase 3 item D)

REGION LOCKS. The save carries per-region campaign state under `state["regions"]` (additive; managed by `PUWRegionState`, `systems/campaign/region_state.gd`): each region has its own record — `champion_beaten`, its own badge list, and the party/boxes that belong to that region. Entering a region you have NOT beaten is a FRESH START: that region's campaign stands alone, and Pokemon from other regions are inaccessible — they are never deleted, they stay stored in their home region's record. Leaving an unbeaten region leaves the Pokemon caught there behind in that region's storage. The gate is the regional CHAMPION: only when a region's Champion is beaten does it join the shared pool — re-entering a beaten region restores the exact team left there, and beaten regions may use each other's Pokemon freely. A region at 99% (everything but its Champion beaten) stays sealed BOTH ways: you cannot import a cleared region's team to finish it, and you cannot export its Pokemon. Precisely: if the active region is beaten, the accessible Pokemon are the active region's own plus every other beaten region's; if the active region is unbeaten, only its own Pokemon are accessible. The structure is region-generic; only Kanto has full game data, so the tests prove the second region (Johto) with a clearly-labeled synthetic record.

GYM SCALING. A gym leader keeps their identity — the exact roster species and their sourced sets — but their levels follow the player's GLOBAL badge count: band `min(badge_count, 7)` of the 8 sourced roster bands (Brock 12-14, Misty 19-21, Surge 22-24, Erika 27-29, Koga 41-43, Sabrina 41-43, Blaine 45-47, Giovanni 48-50), with each mon keeping its offset inside the band. Bands follow the global badge count, not geography: whichever gym you fight first, anywhere, uses the first band. This is the POWER axis; which gyms may be fought at all is the separate AVAILABILITY axis below, and recording a win (`PUWCampaignStitch.record_gym_win`) now goes through that availability gate with no roster-order enforcement.

OBEDIENCE HARD STOP (verified, unchanged): EXP is calculated and stored in full, and a Pokemon's level never rises past `EXPSystem.level_cap_for_badges(current global badge count)` — proven by the protected `tests/exp_cap_tests.gd` (6 passed, 0 failed), whose zero-badge case stores 1,000,000 EXP while the level stops at exactly 10.

## Two-axis gym model (Riley canon, 2026-11-22)

Power and availability are two separate axes, never to be confused. POWER: gym levels and obedience caps scale on the player's GLOBAL badge count — the first gym fought anywhere uses the first-gym band, and the level cap follows the total badge count from every region combined. AVAILABILITY: whether a specific gym may be started at all is REGIONAL and requirement-based, and is never waived by progress in other regions — each gym keeps its own unlock requirements from its region's normal progression (story flags, items, field actions, in-region prerequisites), recorded per gym in `data/gym_requirements.json`. A foreign badge raises your band and cap but opens nothing. A `region_badges` requirement counts only that region's badges; badges from other regions never satisfy it. Riley's example in one sentence: beating badge one in Galar does NOT open Vermilion Gym in Kanto until Kanto's own S.S. Anne / Cut chain requirements for Vermilion are met.

## Global habitat encounters (Phase 3 item E, Riley canon 2026-11-22)

The sourced per-map wild tables (`data/wild_encounters.json`, not shipped in this pack — it already lives in the repo) are no longer species lists: they are the ENVIRONMENT RECORD for each area. `tools/build_area_environments.py` derives `data/area_environments.json` from them (method tags from rate>0 blocks — grass/land to grassland, water/fishing to water, rock_smash to rough-terrain — plus documented name tags for forest/cave/mountain/cold/urban/coastal; 115 of 117 areas tagged, Pokemon Tower 1F/2F honestly untagged and pool-less). `tools/build_species_habitats.py` fetches the PokeAPI CSV dumps and emits `data/species_habitats.json`: HONEST COVERAGE — only 386 of 1,025 species have a non-null PokeAPI habitat; the other 639 join pools by type fit only, nothing invented.

Pools are GLOBAL and flat: an area's pool is every species whose PokeAPI habitat fits the area's environments OR whose typing fits (both fit tables are written in field terms in the header of `systems/overworld/habitat_encounters.gd`), regardless of region or game of origin, with equal odds — pool entries are `{"species": dex id, "slug", "dex_family": "PKDX"|"MDX"}` with NO weights; an MDX counterpart from `data/mdx_mapping.json` is its own entry alongside the PKDX one. Evolution gating: a species reached by a level-up evolution at level L needs party_max >= L (Beedrill: 10); trade evolutions use the documented PUW level-30 branch; all other triggers impose no extra gate beyond the pre-evolution's own. Type rotation is per-area and persisted in the save under the additive `habitat_rotation` key: an encountered species is ineligible in that area until every other pool species sharing at least one of its types has also been encountered there. The encounter RESULT still goes through the existing difficulty path (`PUWTeamBuilder.build_wild_mon`: level = player_max +1..3, IV 31). Roster note: the master registry file in the project holds 25 curated entries, so the 1,025-species roster is merged from `evolution_triggers.json` + `competitive_sets.json` with the registry taking precedence, plus the registry's 3 Gen 10 starters (1026-1028) — 1,028 known species in total. Known gaps: PokeAPI's "rare" habitat maps to no environment (those species enter by type fit only); Mt. Moon and the Seafoam floors get mountain/cold tags but no cave tag, because their names do not say cave and the builder does not guess.
